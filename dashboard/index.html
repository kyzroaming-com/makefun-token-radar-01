How the radar works

Every row is a protocol that records no token in the snapshot and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. That is the watchlist’s own exclusion logic, applied to the whole snapshot instead of only to its $50M floor, so the board ranks all 40 watchlist protocols plus every other protocol that passes the same test.

Implied value is the entry’s own claim, never make.fun’s, and it follows only from the snapshot’s figures:

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier

  • Category multiple: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the snapshot’s live-token protocols in the same category, clamped to 0.05–2.00. A category with fewer than five live comparables uses the global median. The multiples table below shows every category’s multiple and how many live protocols it came from.
  • Growth multiplier: 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −60%, +120%) ÷ 100 × 0.5.
  • Maturity multiplier: 0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.4; a protocol with no listing date is scored at 365 days.

Each farm plan is generated from the protocol’s own record — its category, its chains, its TVL and its age — and every step names what it costs and how long it takes. The plan is a starting point, not advice: a launch is never guaranteed, and the board does not predict whether or when a token is issued.
